# YouTube Content Strategy Playbook for B2B SaaS

## Purpose & Scope

This playbook provides a practical framework for B2B SaaS companies that want to use YouTube as a strategic content and growth channel.

It is based on research collected from practitioners and experts in B2B marketing, YouTube strategy, content distribution, video production, SEO, and demand generation.

The goal is to translate those research findings into practical recommendations, decision frameworks, and repeatable workflows that a B2B SaaS marketing team can apply.
